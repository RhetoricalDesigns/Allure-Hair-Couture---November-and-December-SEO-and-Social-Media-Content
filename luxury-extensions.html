The Allure approach

Made personal.
Never prescribed.

The finest extension work is invisible. Colour, density, placement and method must work together so the result feels balanced from every angle — not simply longer.

Your consultation considers scalp health, lifestyle, natural density and maintenance commitment before a method is recommended. The goal is always believable movement and responsible wear.
